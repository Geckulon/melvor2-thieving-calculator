    How this is worked out

    Average time per attempt = attempt time + (1 − success) × stun chance × stun length. Stun is assumed to come after the failed attempt.

    Drops per second = success × rare-table chance × item share ÷ average time per attempt. The setup with the most drops per second wins. The item share is the same for every setup, so it never changes the winner, only the times.

    Level projections add 1 stealth per level to every setup, up to level 99, recalculate each setup's rate, and report the level where the leader changes. Nothing else is assumed to change as you level.

    XP per hour = success × XP per success × (1 + the setup's thieving XP modifier) ÷ average time per attempt; failures give no XP. Time to a switch adds up each level's XP from the standard XP table, divided by the XP per hour of whichever setup is best at that level, so it rises as your success improves.

    Attempt times are 3s for pickpockets, 6s for stalls and 8s for chests; the stun is 2s. Each upgrade takes 5% off its timer or stun chance. Percentage modifiers of the same kind are added together before they are applied, and perception modifiers scale the target's perception, rounded to the nearest whole number.

    Survival

    Errs on the side of safety: every failure is assumed to hit for the max damage. Ratings use normal play: Danger if you'd die before the average drop, Caution before 99% of players have it, Stable before twice that, Safe beyond it, and Invincible past 24 hours. Any tier whose mark is past 24 hours merges into Invincible, since 24 hours is the offline limit. Deadly whenever your max hit isn't below the auto-eat threshold. You start at full HP with no regeneration, and auto-eat eats one food at a time while you're at or below 25% of max HP (rounded up).
